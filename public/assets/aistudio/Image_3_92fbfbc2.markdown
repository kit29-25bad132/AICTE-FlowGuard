## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, sovereign operational framework engineered for high-stakes regulatory accountability, automated auditing, and governance intelligence. Merging the dense analytical rigor of defense/intelligence operations software with the polished typography and structural clarity of top-tier engineering tools, it rejects decorative fluff in favor of maximum operational efficiency, evidential integrity, and absolute legibility.

### Core Attributes
- **Authoritative & Sovereign:** Evokes statutory authority, procedural fairness, and unyielding trust without feeling bureaucratic or archaic.
- **Forensic Precision:** Micro-typography, fixed tabular alignments, explicit timestamps, cryptographic verification states, and continuous audit trails.
- **Instrumental Density:** Maximizes screen real estate for deep multi-column regulatory ledgers, cross-referencing panes, and telemetry widgets while maintaining strict visual hierarchy.

### Visual Aesthetic
The style follows **Engineered Institutional Modernism**: pristine neutral planes, surgical hairline borders, intentional semantic telemetry accents, and deep navy grounding surfaces.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional authority with high-contrast functional legibility, adhering to strict WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast targets for data verification workflows.

### Color Tiers
- **Primary Navy (`#142B4A`):** Anchors navigation hubs, structural headers, high-level metrics, and sovereign badges. Secondary deep navy (`#203F63`) provides layered containment for intermediate structural elements.
- **Regulatory Action Blue (`#2563EB`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, focal path selections, active entity links, and operational verification triggers.
- **AI Telemetry Purple (`#805AD5`):** Denotes automated regulatory inferences, natural-language document cross-checks, anomaly flags, and synthetic reconciliation tasks.
- **Neutral Framework (`#64748B` base):** Crisp base canvas (`#FFFFFF`), structural container plane (`#F7F9FC`), muted data surface (`#EDF2F7`), and borders (`#E2E8F0`). High-density text relies on ultra-dense carbon (`#0F172A`).

### Compliance & Telemetry Accents
- **Compliant / Verified Green (`#16A36A`):** Validated documentation, approved approvals, cleared quotas.
- **Pending / In-Review Amber (`#E9A23B`):** SLA warning phases, incomplete faculty quotas, awaiting verification.
- **Non-Compliant / Breach Red (`#DC4545`):** Statutory violation, forged affiliation markers, SLA breaches, legal holds.

## Typography

Typography balances systematic readability with institutional authority.

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a crisp, modern, engineered cadence for module headings, operational summaries, and dashboard counters.
- **Body (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Selected for high micro-legibility in tabular grids, nested inspection hierarchies, legal justifications, and deep institutional notes.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to institutional IDs, GIS coordinates, application tracking numbers (e.g., `APP-2026-DL-8831`), timestamps, hash proofs, and status tags.

All numbers in data-dense tables must enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column verticality during live sorting.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a compressed, high-density fluid grid designed to display large volumes of institutional data without horizontal overflow.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Multi-column split-pane master/detail workspace. Left sidebar (260px fixed), primary analytical canvas (flexible 12-column grid, `gutter-desktop: 1rem`), and optional contextual inspection inspector (380px fixed).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible sidebar rail (56px collapsed icon mode), fluid 8-column workspace, drawer-based modal inspection pane.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked workflow, bottom-sheet context inspectors, frozen header controls, and horizontal-scroll data tables with pinned freeze-columns for institution identity.

### Spacing Model
A compact 4px baseline rhythm governs all components. Tables, panels, and forms avoid dead whitespace; operational actions are visually paired with their target entities within `0.25rem` to `0.75rem` bounds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a **flat structural border model** combined with **tonal containment layers** rather than soft diffuse shadows, ensuring legibility under variable display conditions.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure `#F7F9FC` background with structural partitions divided by `#E2E8F0` 1px borders.
- **Workspace Cards & Data Panels (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fills outlined with 1px `#CBD5E1`. Zero blur elevation shadow under resting state.
- **Context Overlays & Dropdowns (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px `#94A3B8` border and an ultra-tight, crisp projection: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.06)`.
- **Statutory Alerts & Critical Inspector Drawers (Level 3):** Grounded `#FFFFFF` plane accompanied by a 1px `#DC4545` (if critical risk) or `#142B4A` outer border and a subtle directional shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses crisp, surgical edges (`roundedness: 1`, 0.25rem / 4px base radius) to maintain structural density.

- **Micro-Badges & Status Chips:** 2px radius or sharp containment tags for strict institutional classification.
- **Inputs, Buttons, & Data Cells:** 4px (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Panels, Master Cards, & Tabbed Views:** 6px to 8px (`0.375rem` - `0.5rem`) outer bounding radius.
- **No Pill Shapes:** Eliminates overly playful pill buttons (`rounded-full`) except for numeric counter markers within audit pipelines.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Actions
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** `#142B4A` background, white label, 1px `#0F172A` border, 4px radius. Height: 32px (compact) or 36px (standard). Pressed: `#0F172A`.
- **Secondary Action:** White background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#142B4A` label. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **AI Automation Action:** Semi-translucent purple tint (`#805AD5` at 10% fill), `#805AD5` border, `#6B46C1` label, with a dedicated micro spark icon.
- **Enforcement / Reject Action:** `#DC4545` outline or solid fill, white or high-contrast red label for SLA revocations.

### Regulatory Data Tables (Linear/Palantir style)
- Compact row height (36px standard, 32px dense).
- Alternating column separation using 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline vertical gridlines.
- Sticky column headers with `#EDF2F7` background, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label-caps (`10px`), and inline tri-state sorting indicators.
- Hover states highlight rows with `#F8FAFC` and reveal persistent micro action bars (View Dossier, Audit Log, Flag Anomaly).

### Status Badges & Compliance Indicators
- **Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Prepended with check-shield glyph.
- **Under Review:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`. Prepended with clock glyph.
- **SLA Breach / Legal Risk:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`. Monospace duration tag (e.g., `+14h 22m`).
- **AI Flagged:** Background `#FAF5FF`, text `#6B46C1`, border 1px solid `#E9D8FD`. Includes confidence index (e.g., `98.4% CONF`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Institutional search inputs include a monospace filter syntax indicator (e.g., `region:west status:breached`) inside the bar.
- Height: 32px, text font `IBM Plex Sans` 13px, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, focus outline 2px solid `#2563EB` with no offset blur.

### Audit Timeline & Ledger Nodes
- Vertical connecting line: 1.5px `#CBD5E1`.
- Step indicator: 8px solid geometric square or dot color-coded to semantic status.
- Timestamp: Monospace right-aligned `JetBrains Mono` 11px string with micro tooltip displaying SHA-256 validation proof.
